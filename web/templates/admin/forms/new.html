Advanced : the JavaScript SDK, special fields

Special fields

Formlander saves every field your form sends, with any name. It reads these fields and does not save them:

_success_url
Where the visitor goes after the form is sent. Use a full URL on one of the allowed sites. Without it, the visitor sees a thank-you page from Formlander.
_error_url
Where the visitor goes when the form is rejected, for example after a failed captcha. Without it, the visitor sees a page from Formlander that explains the problem.
__fl_hp
A trap for robots: a hidden text input. People leave it empty. Robots fill it, and Formlander marks those submissions as spam. The starter code has it.
